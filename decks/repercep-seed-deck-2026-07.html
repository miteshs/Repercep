What you get for everything else

"Here's a GPU-hour and a Dockerfile."

That tail is not small. Every production vision system, industrial inspection line, AV perception stack, robotics fleet, media pipeline, and multi-modal agent lives in it — and it is growing faster than text-only inference, because it is where physical AI lives.

The technique that makes arbitrage sellable

Cross-vendor parity gating

OpenVLA-7B reproduces its own reference implementation to 0.0e+00 maximum error — bit-identical — on both H100 and MI300X. "AMD is 30% cheaper" is a procurement argument. "AMD is 30% cheaper and here is the parity gate on your model" is a product.

Why it isn't trivially copied

A year of production ROCm work across six models and three serving regimes — first published Cosmos benchmark on any AMD GPU — plus a ledger of the traps that cost us months. Durability estimate: 12–24 months. The plan is to be entrenched before it closes.
